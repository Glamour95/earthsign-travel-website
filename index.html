Plan ahead

Festive season fills up first

The tightest window of the year is 20 December to 4 January. Lower Sabie, Satara and Skukuza, and most private lodges, are often full well before then.

If you want a December or January trip, send us your dates now and we'll check live availability before anything sells out.

Check festive dates

Good to know

  • SANParks stays can be booked now through 31 October 2027.
  • The next SANParks booking period opens at reservations offices on 1 December 2026 and online on 2 December 2026.
  • Popular units often go on release day, so we check live inventory for you.
  • No rooms are held and no dates are confirmed until we've checked with the camp or lodge.
